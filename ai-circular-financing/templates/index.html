The five-layer cake: money goes up, purchases come back down

Jensen Huang’s five layers of AI: energy, chips, infrastructure, models, applications. Capital providers such as SoftBank and private credit sit above the cake. Investments, loans and backstops (right) mostly climb the cake; purchase commitments (left) mostly come back down. Where both arrows join the same two layers, the money goes round in a loop.

The BIS evidence: half of AI money comes from other AI firms

The telecom analogue: Nortel, 1996–2015

Late-1990s equipment vendors such as Nortel and Lucent lent to network operators so the operators could buy their gear. When operator revenue fell short, the vendors lost the sales and the loans together. Nortel’s market value, $bn (series from FT Alphaville).
