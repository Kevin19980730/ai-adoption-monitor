Method

Hyperscaler (SEC filings, refreshed when a new 10-Q / 10-K lands): capex = purchases of PP&E; finance leases = ROU assets obtained for finance-lease liabilities; D&A = depreciation of PP&E; CIP = assets not yet in service (Microsoft discloses none); RPO = remaining performance obligations (Meta reports none, so ratios use the four RPO reporters, "ex Meta").

Lab RPO = the part of backlog owed by OpenAI and Anthropic: company disclosures where given (e.g. Microsoft's share of commercial RPO from OpenAI), else signed contract values and Anthropic's S-1/A commitment table. Net RPO = RPO − lab RPO.

Neoclouds & SpaceX AI: CoreWeave and IREN from 10-Q/10-K XBRL, Nebius from its 6-K tables, SpaceX only its AI / Super Intelligence segment (capex and D&A; no AI-only backlog, so no RPO). Their lab RPO: CoreWeave's OpenAI contracts (capped at its 35% largest-customer limit), Nebius and IREN zero on named customers. The "incl. neoclouds" lines only appear for quarters all of them have reported.

End-user spending = Anthropic revenue (S-1/A recognised, else ARR-based) + OpenAI revenue (reported, else ARR-based) + Gemini first-party API (tokens/min × blended price) + open-source model spending (OpenRouter open-weights spend, inference-platform ARR). Vercel AI Gateway publishes shares only; Ramp is a customer sample (memo line).
